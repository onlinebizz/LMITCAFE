# Farfalli site flows

## Guests

- **Home:** Arabic opens by default. English uses `/en`. The persistent header exposes the language switch and menu. Homepage highlights follow availability and the featured setting in the owner workspace.
- **Menu:** Browse pizza, pasta, kitchen and café categories, or search by dish or drink name. Tap a product to open its details. Close with the close button, tap outside the sheet, or press Escape. Items without a published price show a note instead of zero.
- **Visit:** The address, phone and Google Maps link are visible without needing an account. Call to confirm hours until the owner enters the full week. The full menu link opens the restaurant's published FineDine menu. The guide does not take online orders.
- **Counter:** Browse the same catalog with touch, keyboard or category shortcuts. Product details show the full photo where available; close to return to the same scroll position. Auto-scroll is off by default. The owner can turn it on in Café settings. Reduced-motion preferences remain respected.

## Owner

- **Sign-in:** Use the newly provisioned Farfalli owner credentials at `/admin`. This does not sign into the POS or the previous café workspace.
- **Menu and photos:** Add and reorder categories or items, set prices, toggle availability, choose a photo and edit text. Use actual verified product photography.
- **Site settings:** Edit the homepage, contact details, opening hours, announcements and counter display. Leave unknown hours blank. The site shows the weekly schedule only when each day is set or marked closed.
- **Backup:** Download the catalog, promotions and settings from Account. Authentication records are not included. Uploaded media stays in the library.
